# Planning scope and issue lifecycle evaluation

Freeze the [requests](fixtures/linear-issue-lifecycle/tasks.md) and
[criteria](fixtures/linear-issue-lifecycle/criteria.json) before editing skills.
Give five fresh baseline contexts the current issue-writing and wrap-up skills.
These explicit-scope requests already passed. Preserve that result; do not revise
their criteria to manufacture a failure.

Use the separately frozen [design-workflow request](fixtures/linear-issue-lifecycle/design-workflow.md)
and [criteria](fixtures/linear-issue-lifecycle/design-workflow-criteria.json) to
test the interaction with a required design stage and nearby specification issues.
Compare five fresh contexts per arm on that same request, then recheck the five
original boundaries in one fresh candidate context. Evaluators receive no
criteria, desired answers, earlier outputs or design plan.

Read all complete answers. Record demonstrated differences, baseline strengths
and uncertainty. These scenarios compare issue decisions, not automatic skill
selection or authenticated connector behavior. Every service action is proposed
only. Preserve full responses and source fingerprints outside the public repo.

Check both sides of the boundary: a session limited to planning must preserve a
capability's lifecycle, while an explicitly independent research or design issue
can finish at its own accepted deliverable. A title-only correction remains narrow.

Validate package consistency, required repository checks and exact source copies
in disposable portable installs. Keep raw artifacts and private project context
out of published results.
